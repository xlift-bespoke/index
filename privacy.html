Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

This page covers the website you're reading right now – not the data pipelines xLift builds for clients, which are covered separately in your service agreement. Here's the short version: this site doesn't collect anything about you, and we'd like to keep it that way.

What this site loads

This site is static. There's no server-side code processing your visit, no database storing anything about you, and no third-party fonts, analytics, or tracking scripts loaded from anywhere else. Every file the page needs – styling, layout, scripts – is served from the same place as the page itself. Nothing is fetched from a third-party domain when you load a page here.

Cookies and local storage

We don't set cookies, and we don't use browser storage to identify or track you across visits. If your browser stores anything at all while you're here, it's ordinary caching of the page's own files – the same as any website – and it isn't used to build a profile of you.

The contact page

The contact page asks your browser to solve a small computational puzzle before it reveals an email address. That computation runs entirely in your browser, on your device – it isn't sent anywhere, doesn't identify you, and produces no record we keep. It exists purely to make the page less useful to automated scrapers, not to gather anything about you.

Server logs

Whoever hosts this website may keep standard web server logs (things like IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) purely for security and reliability purposes, in line with normal practice for any hosted website. These logs aren't used for marketing, aren't sold, and aren't linked to any other data about you.

If you contact us

If you email us after finding the address on the contact page, we'll use whatever you send us – your message, your email address, any details about your business – only to respond to you and, if you go ahead, to scope and deliver your pipeline. We won't add you to a mailing list or pass your details to anyone else without asking first.

Data handled by a pipeline we build for you

If you become a client, the pipeline itself is a separate matter from this website. As described on the main page, pipelines are built to run on your own device or infrastructure – your business data is processed locally rather than sent to our servers by design. The specifics of any given pipeline, including exactly what it touches and where it runs, are agreed with you directly before any work begins.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Since this is a static site with no accounts or subscriptions, we don't have a way to email you about changes – check back here if you want the latest version.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, the contact page is the way to reach us.
