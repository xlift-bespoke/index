case studies

Company #1 Direct Sync

This company helps other companies get more out of Anaplan.

Problem

Invoices had to move from Xero into Anaplan as line items — exported, reformatted, and re-uploaded by hand every time.

Pipeline

Connects to Xero directly, pulls the invoices, and lands them in Anaplan as ready-to-use line items — no export step, no manual upload.

Company #2 Adaptive

This company buys houses and renovates them.

Problem

Invoices arrived by email from dozens of different tradespeople — PDFs, scanned photos, links to supplier portals, no two formatted the same way — and all needed to reach QuickBooks correctly.

Pipeline

Reads the right inbox folders, pulls every invoice regardless of format, works out what each one actually says, and maps it into QuickBooks against the correct client automatically.
